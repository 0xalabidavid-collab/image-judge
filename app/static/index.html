How this works (click to hide)

An AI editor was asked to do something and gave you two results, A and B. This asks an AI judge which one better does what was asked. You teach it: check its answer, and when it is wrong or unsure, write one sentence on why.

  1. Paste the request, then add the original image(s), Result A and Result B.
  2. Press Evaluate and wait about a minute.
  3. Mark it Correct or Wrong (and say why). That is what it learns from.

Confident = every check agreed. Review = mostly agreed, so check it. Unclear = no verdict, so you decide. Full guide
